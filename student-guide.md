# Demo Gallery — Student Guide

How to get your video into the gallery and comment on other people's videos.

## Adding your video

1. **Upload your video to the shared Drive folder** as **MP4 (H.264 + AAC)**. This is the format browsers play natively — other formats (like the one your phone records by default) will show "No video with supported format" instead of playing.
2. **Add a row to the shared Google Sheet** with three columns:
   - **A — Drive link:** copy the share link from Drive (`https://drive.google.com/file/d/…/view`).
   - **B — Thumbnail timestamp:** the moment in the video the gallery should grab your thumbnail from, in `m:ss` (e.g. `1:23`). Leave blank to use the first frame.
   - **C — Title (optional):** what shows under your thumbnail. Leave blank to show "Video".
3. **Refresh the gallery page.** Your video appears where its row is — the gallery is ordered by sheet row order, and the sheet is the only place you can edit your entry.

## Commenting

- Click **Sign in with Google** and choose your `@ucsc.edu` account. Only UCSC accounts can comment.
- On a video's page, type your comment and post it. Your UCSC email is shown as the comment's author.
- Comments are stored on the video file itself in Google Drive — they're visible both on the site and on the file in Drive.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No video with supported format"** — your file isn't MP4 (H.264 + AAC). Re-export it as MP4.
- **Your video doesn't appear** — check that the link in the sheet points at the video in the shared folder, and refresh the page.
- **Thumbnail shows the wrong moment** — check the timestamp in column B (`m:ss`).
- **Can't sign in** — make sure you're using your `@ucsc.edu` account.
